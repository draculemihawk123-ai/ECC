# Project Overview — Service Connect App

## Product Summary

Service Connect App is a platform that connects customers with suitable service providers through an admin-curated lead assignment flow.

The early product does not use an open marketplace/newsfeed. Instead, admin verifies customer demand, sets lead value in credits, and assigns suitable providers. Providers spend credits to unlock lead details. At most three providers can unlock one lead.

## Target Users

### Customer

Customers create service requests and expect fast, reliable contact from suitable providers.

### Provider

Providers receive assigned leads from admin. They spend credits to unlock verified lead details and contact customers.

### Admin

Admin controls lead quality, provider assignment, credit management, and operational governance.

## Core Value Proposition

- Customers avoid spam and get suitable providers.
- Providers receive curated leads instead of browsing a noisy marketplace.
- Admin maintains quality and controls early-stage marketplace liquidity.

## MVP Scope

Version 0.1 focuses on lead assignment:

1. Customer submits service request.
2. Admin verifies request.
3. Admin sets credit cost.
4. Admin assigns providers.
5. Provider sees assigned lead summary.
6. Provider unlocks lead by spending credit.
7. Lead can be unlocked by maximum three providers.

## Out of Scope for Early MVP

- Public marketplace/newsfeed.
- Fully automatic provider matching.
- Online payment gateway.
- Mobile app.
- Complex review scoring.
- Multi-location/multi-service scaling beyond necessary foundations.

## Success Metrics

- Number of verified leads.
- Lead verification time.
- Provider unlock rate.
- Customer response/contact rate.
- Provider credit consumption.
- Lead refund/complaint rate.
- Ratio of leads reaching 3 accepted providers.
